Where Your Data Goes

What stays on your PC, and what is sent over the internet.

💻

Stays on your PC

  • Extracting audio from the video
  • Transcription (OpenAI Whisper)
  • Creating subtitle files, burning in subtitles and writing the video
  • Translation with a local LLM (LM Studio, Ollama, etc.) running in your own environment
🌐

Sent over the internet

  • Translating with a cloud AI: only the subtitle text is sent to the AI service you choose (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic Claude). Audio and video are not sent
  • Dubbing: the subtitle text to be spoken (the translation, when AI translation is on) is sent to Microsoft's online text-to-speech service (Edge TTS)
  • Downloading a transcription model the first time (from Hugging Face)

Your videos, audio and text are never sent to the developer (Mojosoft). No account signup and no usage tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

FAQ

Straight answers, including what the app cannot do.

Q. Can I translate a YouTube video from its URL?

No. The app does not take URLs. It works with video and audio files on your PC, such as videos you made yourself or videos you have permission to use. If you only want to watch a YouTube video, try YouTube's auto-translated captions.

Q. Does it work offline?

Transcription works offline once you have downloaded a transcription model. Translation can stay in your own environment with a local LLM (LM Studio, Ollama, etc.). Translating with a cloud AI and dubbing (Microsoft's online text-to-speech) need an internet connection.

Q. Is there a subscription?

No. The app is a one-time purchase. If you translate with a cloud AI (Gemini, OpenAI or Claude), that provider may bill you for API usage through your own API key.

Q. Do I need an API key?

Not for transcription, and not for translation with a local LLM. Translating with Gemini, OpenAI or Claude needs an API key from that service. The text-to-speech for dubbing needs no API key, but dubbing into another language uses AI translation.

Q. Can I make bilingual subtitles (original and translation together)?

No. Subtitles come out in one language: the translated language when AI translation is on, otherwise the spoken language.

Q. Can I fix the translation before it becomes subtitles?

Yes. Turn on Review mode to check and edit the subtitle text before burn-in. Review mode only opens when the file list contains a single file (it is skipped when you process two or more files together). You can edit the text, but not the timings.

Q. Can it handle long videos?

The Microsoft Store edition and the purchased download edition have no length limit. The free trial of the download edition handles videos up to 5 minutes each. Processing time depends on the video length and your PC.

Q. Does it run on Mac?

No. It is for Windows (64-bit) only.
