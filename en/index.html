AI translation

AI dubbing

Where Your Data Goes

What stays on your PC, and what is sent over the internet.

Stays on your PC

  • Extracting audio from the video
  • Transcription (OpenAI Whisper)
  • Creating subtitle files, burning in subtitles and writing the video
  • Translation with a local LLM (LM Studio, Ollama, etc.) running in your own environment

Sent over the internet

  • Translating with a cloud AI: only the subtitle text is sent to the AI service you choose (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic Claude). Audio and video are not sent
  • Dubbing: the subtitle text to be spoken (the translation, when AI translation is on) is sent to Microsoft's online text-to-speech service (Edge TTS)
  • Downloading a transcription model the first time (from Hugging Face)

Your videos, audio and text are never sent to the developer (Mojosoft). No account signup and no usage tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

FAQ

Can I add English or other subtitles to my own YouTube videos?

Yes. Download your video from YouTube Studio, open it in the app, and you get subtitle files (SRT, VTT). Add the file in YouTube Studio to publish your video with subtitles.

Does it work on a PC with no internet connection?

Yes. Transcription runs on your PC, and with a local LLM (LM Studio, Ollama, etc.) translation does too, so you can make subtitles offline. You only need the internet to download the models the first time (dubbing uses an online text-to-speech service).

Can I use it for confidential videos?

Yes. Audio and video never leave your PC. With a local LLM, the subtitle text stays in-house too. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Can it handle long videos and many files at once?

Yes. The Microsoft Store edition and the purchased download edition have no length limit. Add several files or a whole folder, and they are processed one after another.

Can I fix the translation before finishing?

Yes. Turn on Review mode to edit the subtitles line by line before burn-in. Review mode opens when the file list contains a single video.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The download edition is free for videos up to 5 minutes, with no time limit and no account.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. Pay ¥2,950 once and keep using it. You choose the translation AI: a local LLM costs nothing extra, and a cloud AI (Gemini, OpenAI or Claude) is billed by that provider.

Can I use the subtitles in other software?

Yes. SRT and VTT are standard subtitle formats that YouTube and most video editors can import.
